Guiding principles
1
Tax-free compounding is the whole point. Every dollar inside this Roth grows permanently free of capital gains tax. Treat idle cash as waste — it earns nothing on the tax-free dimension that matters.
2
Losses inside a Roth are unrecoverable. Unlike a taxable UTMA account, you cannot harvest losses for tax benefit. QFIN was the clearest example — exit losers with no recovery thesis immediately.
3
15-year horizon changes everything. Defensive sector tilts (staples, utilities) belong in a retiree's Roth. Liam's Roth should be maximum-growth oriented — SPHQ + FTEC + NVDA is the right spine.
4
RSI >70 means patience, not action. Set price alerts at logical support levels. Don't chase recovery rallies. The entry points will come — and buying right costs nothing in a long-horizon account.
5
Add to winners on every contribution cycle. Each new annual contribution goes to SPHQ first, FTEC second, then NVDA on any pullback. Don't introduce new names without a clear 15-year thesis.
